Most AI roadmaps fail in one of two directions. They are either wish lists
disconnected from the data that would make them possible, or they are so
cautious that nothing ships. A useful roadmap sits in the middle: grounded in
what your data can support today, honest about what needs building first, and
sequenced so early wins fund the harder work.

I run a focused assessment of your data reality, your team's capability, and
your business goals, then write the plan down: what to build first, what to
measure, and what to defer without guilt.

Good fit if you are accountable for an AI outcome this year and need a plan
your engineers will not quietly route around.
