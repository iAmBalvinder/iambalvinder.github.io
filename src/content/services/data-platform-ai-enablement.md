Your data platform took years to get right: tested pipelines, governed access,
trusted reporting. AI features that bypass it and scrape PDFs into a vector
database throw all of that away, then fail quietly when the underlying data
goes stale.

This engagement connects your existing platform to your AI ambitions. We add
the layers AI workloads actually need - quality gates on model-facing sources,
a semantic or retrieval layer over the warehouse, and synchronization
pipelines that keep indexes fresh - while keeping the governance you already
have.

Good fit if you lead a data team and want AI to be an extension of the
platform, not a shadow stack running beside it.
